### Translation capabilities exercised by the maintained cases

The integration work uncovered and tested real constructs including:

- Palette-based colouring, negative palettes and map-style XYZ plots.
- Gnuplot circles/arcs, rectangles, filled curves and scalar expressions.
- OHLC candles, volume panels, multiplot and SI axis tick formatting.
- Inline XYZ data, `dgrid3d`, contour drawing, `pm3d` and heatmap rendering.
- `set size ratio` and `set for [...] linetype ... ps ...`.
- Named axis tics in combination with modifiers such as `nomirror`.
- Styled and aligned labels (`font`, `tc rgb`, `left`, `center`, `right`) and
  graph-relative label coordinates.
- Horizontal `xerrorbars` and associated `lc black` style handling.
- `set style line` with omitted `lw`, including `lc rgb` / `lt` combinations
  and standalone `ps` / `pointsize` modifiers.
- Arithmetic scalar expressions in `set offsets`, categorical `ytic(1)`
  labels and `with boxxy` rectangles used by `random_forest`.
- Vertical and horizontal `gridplot` layouts generated by `tsdecomp`,
  and Matplotlib marker sizing for `fsboost` coefficient paths.

Translation/parser changes were accompanied by dedicated tests within
`gptranslate`. The earlier 15 November full Meson regression passed **83/83**;
a **later full regression was reported as passing on 17 November** after the
`fsboost` point-size correction, but its exact count was not supplied. The
maintained hansl fixtures were updated separately when failures stemmed
from test data or call signatures. No fix required modifying libgretl or
upstream package source code; Brown was updated to an existing later release.

The `LocalProjection` integration log also showed an upstream deprecation
warning about legacy `--band` syntax; this was **not** a test failure and
is not evidence of a `gptranslate` regression.

## Interpretation and limitations

Static scanning is heuristic and does not prove that a function's plotting
path is reachable through its public API. `NO_SAMPLES` and `NO_FIGURES` are
not equivalent to absent graphing functionality. The 32-case maintained integration
suites and the 56 successful candidate examples verify runtime translation
and figure creation, but neither assures pixel-identical output relative to
gnuplot. The **zero-FAIL** result is qualified by the explicit skip policies
for `.bin` databases and missing `.inp` includes, and by the permanent
exclusion of generated `session.inp` files.
